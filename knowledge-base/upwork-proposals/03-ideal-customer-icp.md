# 03 — Ideal Customer (ICP)

## One-line ICP

**Founder- or CEO-led company ($1M–$50M revenue range) that has already decided AI is strategic and now needs a builder to deploy it — not a consultant to convince them it matters.**

## Hard qualifiers (agent should look for these in the job post)

Strong green flags:

- ✅ Founder, CEO, COO, or "Head of" is writing the job post (not a junior ops person).
- ✅ They describe a *specific workflow* they want improved (lead response, outbound, inbox, scheduling, reconciliation, recruiting, QA, reporting).
- ✅ They mention Claude, Anthropic, OpenAI, agents, agentic workflows, RAG, LangChain, or similar — shows they're past the "what is AI" stage.
- ✅ They've tried tooling already (ChatGPT subscriptions, Zapier, Make, n8n, custom GPTs) and hit a wall.
- ✅ Budget is five figures or hourly rate is realistic for senior work ($100+/hr).
- ✅ They describe a recurring, multi-step job — not a one-time script.
- ✅ Industry fit: real estate brokerages, B2B SaaS, professional services, financial services, healthtech, recruiting firms, consulting, agencies — anything ops-heavy with repeatable workflows.

## Soft qualifiers (nice to have)

- Client history on Upwork with good reviews and real spend.
- Timezone overlap with Dubai (EMEA and US East Coast are easiest).
- English fluency (ICP leans North American + GCC + Western Europe).

## Hard disqualifiers (do not bid — or decline nicely)

- ❌ Budget under $500 total for custom build work.
- ❌ Hourly cap below $50/hr.
- ❌ "Need a ChatGPT expert" with no specific workflow.
- ❌ "Generate 100 blog posts with AI" / pure content-mill jobs.
- ❌ "Clone ChatGPT" / "build an AI like GPT-4" — unrealistic asks.
- ❌ MLM, crypto pump, "AI" affiliate funnels.
- ❌ Requests to use the account for arbitrage or white-labeling Luka's identity.
- ❌ Legally or ethically ambiguous use cases (scraping protected data, impersonation, automated fraud).

## Ideal job post patterns (examples)

If the agent sees posts like these, they're probably a fit:

- "We're a real estate brokerage with 30 agents. Lead response is slow. We want an AI that qualifies leads the moment they come in and routes to the right agent."
- "Looking for an AI engineer to build an agent that reads inbound investor emails and drafts replies in our voice."
- "We have an ops team drowning in contract admin. Want to explore using Claude or GPT to automate the 80%."
- "Need someone senior to scope and build our first production AI agent. Seen lots of demos — need someone who has actually deployed."
- "Head of Product at a Series A — want a hands-on advisor to stand up our first AI workflow."
- "Need AI training / workshop for our leadership team." (precedent: the 3-day Dubai training that shipped successfully)

## Red-flag language

Be cautious if the post includes:

- "Just need a prompt written"
- "Very simple, should take 1-2 hours"
- "Looking for cheapest provider"
- "Must sign NDA before we tell you anything" (often low-trust clients)
- "Revenue share only" (no upfront)
- "Need 50 AI agents built by Friday" (scope/timeline disconnect)

## Real-estate sub-ICP (dedicated vertical)

This is our biggest-investment vertical. If the lead is a **real estate brokerage**:

- 15–200 agents
- $50K–$5M monthly GCI
- Pain points to probe:
  - Lead response time (industry avg 30+ min → we get to <5 min, unlocking 21× qualification rate)
  - Transaction coordination (15–20 hrs/week of manual admin)
  - Recruiting (60-day avg hiring cycle)
  - Commission reconciliation (3–5% revenue leakage)
- Direct them to `naironai.com/for/real-estate` and offer the cost-savings calculator as a soft next step before the call.

## Profile of the buyer we do *not* want

- Early founder pre-revenue with no clarity on what they want built.
- Enterprise procurement with 3-month legal review before anything ships.
- Solo operator who wants a ChatGPT wrapper and calls it an "AI employee."
- Agencies trying to subcontract us at 3× markup.

## Fit-check question the agent can ask

If ICP is unclear from the post, the agent can ask in the first reply:

> "Quick qualifier so I don't waste your time — what's the workflow you want the AI to own, and is there a human on your team who'll be the 'owner' of this system once it's live?"

The answer tells you 80% of what you need to know.
