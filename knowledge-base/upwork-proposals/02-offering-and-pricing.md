# 02 — Offering & Pricing

## The core offer (the thing we actually sell)

**Custom-built AI employees, deployed into the client's real workflow, operated on our infrastructure, and continuously improved.**

This is an **ongoing partnership**, not a one-off build. We don't hand over code and leave — the AI employee lives on our hardware and in our platform (Hive), and we keep tuning it as frontier model capabilities evolve.

## What's included in a standard engagement

| Phase | What happens | Timeline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Identify | Deep dive into daily operations. Map workflows. Pick the highest-leverage first AI employee. | Week 1 |
| Audit | Technical audit of existing stack. Design the integration architecture. | Week 1–2 |
| Implement | Engineers build, test, and deploy the AI employee on dedicated hardware + Hive. | Days 2–3 |
| Optimize | Weekly operating rhythm. Bi-weekly strategy sessions. Quarterly KPI planning. | Ongoing |

**First AI employee live in ~3 days.**

## "First AI employee free" offer (lead magnet)

This is the hero offer on the website and the hook the agent can lean on for qualified Upwork leads:

- ✅ Free audit
- ✅ Free onboarding
- ✅ Implementation fee waived
- ✅ Bi-weekly strategy sessions
- ✅ 1 month of uninterrupted operations included
- ✅ First **$500/month of token costs** covered by Nairon

Client only pays for:
- Dedicated hardware (Mac Mini in our data center)
- LLM token usage above the $500/mo baseline
- The ongoing service/retainer once v1 is operating and validated

**When to offer it on Upwork:** Only to qualified leads (see `03-ideal-customer-icp.md`). Do not offer it to every inbound — it's a real investment on our side and only makes sense when the lead matches ICP and has an AI mandate.

## Pricing logic (for the agent's reference — do not quote fixed numbers on Upwork)

- **Upwork hourly profile rate:** $150/hr (this is what's on the profile; used for smaller scopes, consultations, training gigs).
- **Discovery call consultation:** $25–$45 for a 30–60 min call (used on Upwork to filter serious leads).
- **Training / workshops:** Fixed-price, e.g. $600 for multi-day classroom training (precedent: "AI-Powered Architecture & Design" 3-day training in Dubai).
- **MVP / fixed-price projects:** Starting around $2–3K for scoped custom GPT/RAG work (precedent: $2,500 "Custom Chatbot with RAG for Knowledge Bases" project catalog offering; $2,030 AI Edtech ERP MVP).
- **Ongoing AI employee retainer:** Custom pricing set after discovery. Typically includes hardware lease + token overage + service fee. **Never quote this number in an Upwork proposal** — always drive to a discovery call to scope.

## How to handle pricing in Upwork messages

1. **Don't drop a number in the first message.** Drop value, drive to a call.
2. If the lead insists on a range, give a range tied to scope (e.g., "For a consultation-only engagement, I bill at $150/hr. For a custom AI system, scope and retainer get set after a 15-min discovery").
3. **Never undercut the profile rate.** If a lead wants someone under $50/hr, they're the wrong client.
4. If the job post has a fixed budget well below value, redirect: "Happy to scope what's realistic inside that budget on a 15-min call."

**Operations & delivery AI employees**
- Knowledge (team memory)
- CRM (keep records clean)
- Reporting (weekly KPI summaries)
- QA (check output, flag risk)
- Coordination (handoffs + status)

**Oversight layer**
- Operator alerts in Slack
- Approval checkpoints before sensitive actions

## Things to never promise

- "Replace your whole team" — we augment, we don't replace, and saying otherwise scares buyers.
- Specific ROI numbers we haven't verified with a real client.
- Over-promising an unrealistically fast build when the integration is actually complex — our default is ~3 days for v1, but we scope honestly when a client's stack demands more.
- "Full automation" with no human checkpoints — that's not how we operate.
