## Reference Job 1 — AI Architect for Marketing Systems ("team of AI employees")

**Headline:** AI Architect Needed to Build "AI Employees" for Marketing Systems (SEO, Content, Research)

**Snapshot:**
- Hourly: $30–$60, Expert, <1 week, <30 hrs/week
- Client: Hero Marketing (operator-led marketing agency for home service companies)
- Spend: $7K+, 5-star, payment verified, USA
- Proposals when we saw it: 50+

**Their stated problem (paraphrased):**
> We're hiring an AI Architect / Systems Builder to design and deploy a **team of "AI employees"** powering our in-house marketing and content engine. Not a basic prompt engineering role. We've already built heavily in proprietary Claude-based market research frameworks — SEO content systems (service pages, city pages, blogs), operator-led strategy built for home service companies. The overarching framework for "what" we need is built — we're productizing and scaling it into an AI-driven system. Want a structured system of "roles" that function like a team:
> - AI Market Research Analyst
> - AI Content Strategist
> - AI SEO Architect (service pages, city hubs)
> - AI Blog Writer (long-form + GEO/AEO optimized)
> - AI QA / Editor (brand voice, structure, accuracy)
> Agents should follow defined workflows, build on each other's outputs, maintain consistency across large-scale content production, integrate with Google Docs, Notion, CMS platforms. Scope of Work: architect multi-agent AI system (Claude / OpenAI / hybrid), structure + refine prompt frameworks, build memory/context systems for consistency, design pipelines for market research → strategy → content → QA, recommend tools (LangChain, APIs, automation tool, etc.), create repeatable systems deployable across clients + Asana workflow software, help think through how to turn this into long-term competitive advantage.

**What they're NOT looking for:**
- Basic ChatGPT users
- Generic prompt writers
- Theoretical AI consultants

**What they want:**
- Real builder who understands systems, scale, output quality, business impact
- Multi-agent AI workflows experience
- Strong LLM understanding (Claude, GPT, etc.)
- LangChain, LlamaIndex, Zapier, Make, automation pipelines
- Vector databases / memory systems
- SEO/content experience (big plus)
- Translating business goals into technical architecture

**Why we want this:**
- They literally use our language ("AI employees," "team")
- Claude-based — exactly our stack
- They've done the hard thinking; they just need a builder
- Mid-market client with budget and clarity

**How the agent should bid:**
- Template A is the closest fit.
- **Do not** pitch Nairon's "AI employees" as if they need to learn it — they already use the term.
- Echo their framing back; diagnose the QA + memory layer as the hard part; credential-anchor with Claude multi-agent experience; one qualifying question; CTA to call.
- Rate: within their $30–$60 hourly band is below profile, but this is the kind of engagement Luka would take on to win a logo. Alternative: respond at their rate but steer on the call toward a fixed-scope engagement that maps to Nairon's model.

**One-line scoring:** This is a 14/15 lead — push hard.

## Reference Job 2 — AI Automation Contractor for Property Management

**Headline:** AI Automation Contractor — Build Production Workflows for Property Management Company

**Snapshot:**
- Hourly: $35–$100, Expert, 3–6 months, <30 hrs/week
- Client: 22-year-old commercial property management, 11 employees, 112 clients, CA/NV/TX/FL
- Spend: $10K+, 5-star, payment verified, USA
- Proposals when we saw it: <5 (fresh post)

**Their stated problem (paraphrased):**
> We're rebuilding our operations with AI automation. Looking for an experienced contractor to take ownership of building, deploying, and maintaining AI-powered workflows. **Not a strategy or advisory role.** We need a builder who can ship. Scope:
> - Build AI automations against pre-documented SOPs and workflows
> - Integrate Claude/Cowork with existing tools (Microsoft 365, Appfolio, Laserfiche)
> - Debug, maintain, and iterate on deployed automations
> - Write clear technical documentation so workflows can be operated by non-technical staff
> - Monitor new AI tools and surface ones worth our evaluation

**Required:**
- 12 months hands-on with LLMs (Claude, GPT, or equivalent) in production environments
- Demonstrated track record of shipped automations with measurable business outcomes (must provide examples)
- Experience integrating AI with business software via APIs and webhooks
- Able to explain technical decisions to non-technical stakeholders
- Strong written communication

**Nice to have:**
- Microsoft 365 APIs, Appfolio, Laserfiche
- Prior work with property management, real estate, B2B services companies
- Claude Skills
- Designing multi-step agent workflows

**Engagement:**
- Start 15–20 hrs/week, contractor basis
- 3-month initial engagement, extendable based on performance
- Remote, async-friendly, Pacific Time preferred but not required

**Proposal requirements (hard filter — must answer all):**
1. Links or descriptions of 2–3 automations built, including the business outcome each produced
2. One sentence on what you think is overrated in the current AI space
3. One sentence on what you think is underrated
4. Any questions about scope (encouraged)
> Proposals without those 3 pieces of information will not be reviewed. Shortlisted candidates will be invited to a paid trial project before ongoing engagement.

**Why we want this:**
- Serious client (22 years, $10K+ Upwork spend, clear about what they need)
- 3-month engagement = runway to demonstrate value and extend
- Pacific Time async — workable from Dubai
- Real enterprise stack integrations (MS365, Appfolio, Laserfiche) — Luka has analogous experience
- They explicitly want a builder, which matches how Luka operates

**How the agent should bid:**
- Template B is the exact fit.
- **Follow the proposal requirements exactly.** Answer 1, 2, 3, 4 in order. No fluff.
- Past automations must be real and from `05-proof-case-studies.md` (Rhetora AI, CodeGPT advisory, Smartsy, Keylead, Questionbase growth work, the Dubai training is *not* an automation).
- Overrated / underrated lines: keep short, senior, and defensible — see `08-proposal-writing-playbook.md` for the approved list.
- **Do not** pitch Nairon, AI employees, 3-day timelines, Hive, or hardware.
- Ask *one* real question back — ideally about which SOP is most painful today (surfaces where v1 should live without us saying "v1").
- Close with the calendar link.

**One-line scoring:** This is a 15/15 lead — highest-priority bid.

## The pattern across target jobs

Both reference jobs share a common shape — use this to identify similar posts in the wild:

### Green-flag job attributes

- **Founder/operator is writing the post** (not a junior researcher or an agency procurement rep)
- **Budget signal is real** ($5K+ hourly range or $1K+ fixed scopes; spend history on Upwork visible)
- **They name specific tools** (Claude, OpenAI, LangChain, Notion, Asana, Microsoft 365, Appfolio, Laserfiche, vector DBs)
- **They name specific roles/workflows** for the AI (analyst, strategist, writer, QA, automation against SOPs) — not "use AI somehow"
- **They've done homework** — they know what they don't want ("not prompt writers," "not consultants," "not demos")
- **They want production-grade outcomes** — measurable business impact, documentation, monitoring
- **They explicitly want a builder**, not an advisor
- **Multi-step / multi-agent** workflow language appears
- **3+ month engagements** or productizable scope (long-term revenue shape)

### Red-flag job attributes (the opposite — do not bid)

- "Simple ChatGPT prompts," "1-hour task," "should be easy"
- Budget under $500 total or hourly cap below $50
- Vague language with no specific workflow
- "Revenue share only" / pay-after-results-only
- MLM, crypto, affiliate funnel, content mill
- Requires signing an NDA before learning what the job is
- "Need AI like ChatGPT / GPT-5" (unrealistic asks)
- Agencies trying to subcontract

### The "magic phrase" list (bid faster if you see these)

- "AI employees" / "team of AI agents" / "AI workforce"
- "Claude-based" / "Anthropic"
- "Production-grade" / "ship" / "real deployments"
- "Multi-agent" / "agentic workflows"
- "Measurable business outcomes"
- "Not theoretical" / "not slideware"
- "Integrated with [real enterprise tools]"
- "Long-term / retained / ongoing"

These match our positioning almost 1:1. Prioritize them.
