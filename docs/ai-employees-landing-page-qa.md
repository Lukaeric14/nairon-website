# Landing Page Q&A

Messaging source for homepage FAQ-style sections, VSL scripting, and sales collateral.

## How do you operate?

Week one is a full audit of your business operations so we can identify the highest-leverage opportunity for an AI employee.

Week two is where our team builds the AI employee and deploys version one into your actual workflow.

Week three is focused on stress-testing. We look for edge cases, tighten guardrails, validate approvals, and make sure nothing important is slipping through cracks.

Week four is where the system moves into an operational rhythm with reporting, iteration, and ongoing optimization.

## I’ve already worked with AI companies before. What makes you different?

Most AI companies stop at demos, wrappers, or scattered automations. We focus on building AI employees that own a specific role inside the business.

That means we start with leverage, not novelty. We define KPIs, deploy the system into real operations, and stay involved so the AI employee keeps improving after launch.

## Is your team in-house or do you outsource your engineers?

Our team is in-house. We do not run client work through outsourced engineering shops.

As we scale, we are hiring from a broader community of AI engineers we already know and rate highly for both technical ability and communication. But the work itself stays under our internal team, standards, and accountability.

## Can you build on top of tools I’m already using?

Usually yes. The goal is to make the AI employee operational inside your current stack, not force you into a full migration unless the stack itself is the bottleneck.

## How do you handle strict compliance or sensitive data?

We design around the constraint instead of pretending it does not exist.

That means role-scoped permissions, approval layers for higher-risk actions, auditability, and a deployment plan that respects the data boundaries your business already has to operate within.

Where it makes sense, we use platforms like Composio as the integration layer so credentials and tool access stay controlled instead of being handed over broadly.

## What does the security model actually look like?

We keep production agents inside clear operating boundaries:

- Scoped integrations instead of shared root access
- Approval checkpoints before sensitive actions
- Replayable logs for outputs, tool calls, and failures
- Dedicated infrastructure so the runtime is stable and monitored

This is also why we use dedicated Mac minis running 24/7 for browser-based or always-on work. The system should not depend on someone’s laptop staying open.

## Who pays for token usage?

We cover up to $500 per month in token usage. After that, usage is billed directly to the client so costs stay transparent as volume grows.

## How much does it cost?

Every engagement is tailored to the company we are working with.

Before we onboard a client, we run:

- A discovery call to understand what the business needs and where the highest-leverage opportunities are.
- An audit call to review systems, propose a solution, and map the implementation roadmap.

This gives both sides clarity on deliverables, target KPIs, scope, and what starting the engagement would actually look like.

## Do you work on a project basis or a subscription basis?

We operate on a subscription basis, with quarterly planning around KPIs, implementations, and optimization priorities.

AI employees are operational systems, so they need an ongoing improvement loop rather than one-off project energy.

## What do touchpoints look like each week?

We run weekly strategy calls, monthly status check-ins, and quarterly planning meetings to stay aligned on progress and priorities.

Day-to-day communication happens in Slack so decisions and feedback stay close to the work.

## What do you need from me during the process?

We need access to the right workflows, one decision-maker who can approve direction quickly, and one operator close to the function we are improving.

We do not need your team to become technical experts. We need context, access, and fast feedback so the AI employee can be deployed properly.
