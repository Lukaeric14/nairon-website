1. **Canvas:** warm paper body + cinematic dark hero (Legora structure).
2. **Kept assets:** Nairon logo + Lapis blue trio (#1378E6 / #091F40 / #4A9CFF). Geist available via Hive continuity.
3. **People:** anonymized only — silhouettes, hands, backs. No identifiable faces; Luka does not appear in site imagery (amends Module 70: founder presence = signed vision + LinkedIn, not photography).
4. **Imagery doctrine — "the Tesla grammar"** (from Luka's three Tesla Solar screenshots, verbatim requirement: "close ups like how Legora did, but authentic to those industries. LIKE TESLA"):
   - Real environments, lived-in and warm; work happening blurred/anonymized around a calm precise element.

   - Banned: jobsite gimmicks — "no happy man on a jobsite checking things off his iPad" (verbatim); hard-hat stock; smiling-at-camera.
