# Nairon — Strategy (Phase 0)

Status: **LOCKED** — Gate 1 signed by Luka 2026-07-13. Changes only on Luka's explicit instruction.
Source: brand discovery interview 2026-07-13 (`brand/discovery/`), docs.naironai.com, hive repo.

## 1. What Nairon is

Two arms, one company:
- **Nairon Consultancy** — finds one painful job, builds one AI employee, deploys it hands-on, proves it with a number.
- **Hive** — the workspace where humans and AI employees work together: channels, approvals, audit trail, routines. Industry-agnostic, always.

Win state (5yr): the platform company — with other consultancies performing service on their clients through Hive. Nairon's consultancy is the reference implementation of the model the platform sells.

## 2. Positioning

For property management operators — leasing teams, maintenance & dispatch, rent & renewals — 50–150 employees, $3M–$10M+ revenue, an ops leader or owner holding problem, budget, and authority — who are about to close an operations gap with another hire, an in-house AI engineer, or another SaaS tier: **Nairon builds AI employees that own real jobs and prove it with published numbers.** Unlike AI agencies (no platform) or vertical SaaS (no humans), Nairon is both arms in one company.

- Category term: **AI employees**. Never: bot, chatbot, copilot, assistant, automation tool.
- Only-we claim: consultants who own the outcome + a supervision-built platform we own.
- Price posture: **accessible premium** — premium mechanics, zero exclusivity signals, priced for the land grab.

## 3. Vertical (canon, 2026-08-04 revision, revised again same day — supersedes the solar-only canon and the prior 7-vs-3 discrepancy)

**Property management only.** The site, film, and imagery show property management exclusively — no solar, no HVAC, no other vertical, featured or quiet. Every prior placeholder vertical (Logistics & 3PL, Franchises, Healthcare Staffing, Software) stays dropped.

**Three sub-niches within property management** — distinct workflow, same vertical, same buyer's org:
- **Leasing** — fill vacancies. Pain: inquiries answered around the clock, applicant pre-qualification, tour scheduling, follow-up until move-in.
- **Maintenance & Dispatch** — keep units running. Pain: triaging resident work orders, matching and dispatching the right vendor, tracking every job to closed. Proven (flagship number below).
- **Rent & Renewals** — keep the money and the lease coming in. Pain: chasing overdue rent, sending renewal offers on schedule, flagging delinquencies before they cost a month.

Hive itself is never verticalized: vertical expression lives in consultancy pages, case studies, and imagery only.

## 4. Audience

- **ICP:** mid-size operator, 50–150 staff, ≥$3M revenue (ideal $10M+), committee-free buying. Buyer = the person with the problem and the budget (ops leader; sometimes owner).
- **Anti-ICP:** enterprise procurement processes; automation bargain hunters; buyers comparing us to offshore engineers; custom-software requests outside AI-employee scope.
- **Primary objection to answer on every page:** "this is too advanced for us." Standing answers: no new software, no migrations, we do the setup, approvals on everything.
- Secondary objections: "AI will embarrass me" (→ approvals + audit trail), "I got burned before" (→ numbers + own-failures honesty), "my team will reject it" (→ humans + AI side by side; adoption is our job).

## 6. Brand argument, personality, voice

**The argument:** (1) self-serve products will win simple workflows; complicated operational work needs humans to discover, set up, deploy, monitor. (2) Autonomy is earned per job — every rushed agent underwhelms; approvals and audit trails are product. (3) Therefore both arms, one company.

**Personality:** the proven young operator who communicates like a lawyer. Competence first, excitement second, sophistication as finish. Anti-model: ServiceTitan (dated, dumbed-down, mass-market). Rule: digestible, never dumbed down — the buyer is treated as an early mover in their industry.

**Emotional engine:** "AI is the new internet, I can't get left behind" — era urgency, delivered calmly through evidence.

**Voice bans (absolute):** hype vocabulary; bot/chatbot/copilot; exclamation marks; unexplained tech terms. Confirmed on-voice phrases: "AI employees own outcomes, not steps" · "controlled autonomy, not blind autonomy" · "no new software, no migrations."
