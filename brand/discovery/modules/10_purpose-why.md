## Synthesis

**Core purpose (three candidate formulations):**
1. Define how humans and AI employees work together — correctly: supervised, accountable, measured.
2. Give operating businesses hire-level AI leverage with employee-level accountability.
3. Be the company that made AI employment real before the category had rules.

**The strategic shape:** two clocks run at once. The consultancy is durable (professional services need no market share); the platform is a race (industry-agnostic humans+agents workspace that must reach critical mass before vertical clones appear). The website serves the consultancy clock now — audit CTA — while the brand must not paint the platform into a vertical corner.

**Belief system (the argument the brand makes):** simple workflows will be won by self-serve products; complicated operational workflows need humans to discover, set up, deploy, and monitor — and autonomy must be earned per job, because unsupervised agents fail. Nairon is the company built around both facts: consultants who own outcomes, on a platform built for supervision (approvals, audit trails).

**Proof register (usable today):** Solar permits $125 → $25 including labor; property management 150 → 250 doors per manager (target, engagement closing); department-level case studies from Keylead (content production up, costs down), Question Base (revenue up), CodeGPT (upsell agents), NEOM. HVAC: no traction and no written source — it is featured on direction, not evidence.

- HVAC is on the featured list with zero current proof or pipeline mentioned — accepted risk or placeholder?
- "Autonomy is earned" incident story not yet told — needed for founder narrative.
- Emotional register: "building the future of work" (definitional ambition) must coexist with the traction-led, numbers-first sales reality. The brand voice needs both: category confidence, court-filter proof.
