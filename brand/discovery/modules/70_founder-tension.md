# Module 70 — Founder Brand vs Company Brand

Participant: Luka (CEO). Date: 2026-07-13.

## Raw

**Luka's role:** "The face" — founder-led brand. His name signs the vision, he appears in content, LinkedIn is a channel, buyers know who they're dealing with.

**Win state (verbatim):** "The platform company — with a caveat: we believe that other consultancies like Nairon will use our platform to perform service on their clients."

## Synthesis

**Founder-brand decision:** stronger presence than the Legora model (which is manifesto + one photo). Consequences: a signed vision section on the site; Luka may appear in the film's human moments; site copy can use first person plural with an identifiable author; LinkedIn content is part of the brand system and must follow the same voice doctrine.

**The platform endgame, stated precisely:** Hive's win condition is not only end-customers — it is consultancies performing service on their clients through Hive. Nairon's consulting arm is therefore the first proof of the operating model the platform sells. Brand consequences:
- The vertical expression (Solar/HVAC/PM) must live in the consultancy's pages, imagery, and case studies — never in the platform's identity, naming, or diagram. Hive is presented industry-agnostic.
- The stack diagram (already committed, Module 40 batch) doubles as the future partner-facing story.
- Two audiences read the same site in different years: operators today, consultancies later. Nothing in the brand should need demolition for the second reading.

**Tension to manage:** founder-led + platform-destined. Handled by scoping the founder's face to the era argument and the consultancy, not to the platform's identity.
