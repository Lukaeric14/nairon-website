# Module 40 — Personality & Archetype

Participant: Luka (CEO). Date: 2026-07-13.

## Raw

**Walk-in persona:** "The proven young operator" — sharp, fast, has done this exact deployment 50 times; confidence from reps, not age; slightly impatient with old ways. (Offered and not chosen: trade-fluent engineer, calm senior consultant, steady advisor.)

**Anti-model (verbatim):** "ServiceTitan I feel hits the spot but it's very old style. You feel like they made their product dumb for their audience. We need to make our content digestible but not so old... it needs to feel more exciting than ServiceTitan. ServiceTitan feels like it's for the masses. Our audience should feel like: AI is the new internet, I can't get left behind."

## Synthesis

**Personality formula:** the proven young operator who communicates like a lawyer (see Module 50). Precision without age; energy without hype.

**The ServiceTitan lesson (binding):** digestible ≠ dumbed down. ServiceTitan's failure in Luka's eyes is condescension ("made their product dumb for their audience") and datedness ("for the masses"). Nairon treats the operator as smart and ambitious — the Legora move ("exceptional lawyers") translated: this buyer is an early mover in their industry, and the brand should tell them so.

**The emotional engine:** urgency of a new era — "AI is the new internet, I can't get left behind" — delivered calmly. Not fear-mongering; the quiet confidence that the shift is already underway and the reader can be on the right side of it. This is the hero film's core feeling and the hero section's job.

**Aaker dimensions (ranked):** 1. Competence (proof, precision, audit trails). 2. Excitement (new-era energy, speed, modernity) — the differentiator; Legora and premium peers run competence + sophistication, ServiceTitan runs neither; competence + excitement is open ground. 3. Sophistication (the finish, never the lead — "accessible premium").

**Archetype blend:** Hero (winning the race, reps, outcomes) tempered by Sage (lawyer-grade precision, evidence). Avoid: Magician framing (magic = black box = the intimidation trigger), Ruler framing (exclusivity contradicts accessible premium).

**Three candidate personality statements:**
1. The proven operator of the AI era: young, precise, already done it fifty times.
2. New-era energy, courtroom discipline.
3. The firm that treats a roofing company like an early mover, not a laggard to be sold to.
