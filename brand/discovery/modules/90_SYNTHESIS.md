# 90 — Synthesis: The Nairon Brandbook

Source: interview of Luka (CEO), 2026-07-13, modules 10–70. Method: batched AskUserQuestion. Status: draft pending Gate 1 sign-off via `brand/STRATEGY.md`.

## Purpose

Define how humans and AI employees work together — supervised, accountable, measured. The conviction comes from three years of first-hand agent deployments: every rushed agent underwhelmed. The drive is definitional: AI employment is arriving; Nairon intends to be the company that set its standards.

## Positioning

**For** operators in Solar, HVAC, and Property Management (50–150 employees, $3M–$10M+ revenue, ops leader or owner with problem, budget, and authority in one person) **who** are about to close an operations gap with another hire, an in-house AI engineer, or another SaaS tier, **Nairon** builds AI employees that own real jobs and prove it with published numbers, **unlike** AI agencies (no platform, no accountability) or vertical SaaS (no humans, promises without deployment), **because** Nairon is both arms in one company: the consultants who own the outcome and the platform (Hive) it runs on.

- **Category term:** AI employees. Banned category terms: bot, chatbot, copilot, automation tool, assistant.
- **Verticals:** three featured (Solar, HVAC, Property Management); four served quietly (Logistics, Franchises, Healthcare Staffing, Software).
- **Price posture:** accessible premium — premium mechanics, no exclusivity signals; priced to win the land grab.
- **Anti-ICP:** procurement mazes; automation bargain hunters; anyone comparing Nairon to offshore engineers; custom-software requests outside AI-employee scope.

## The argument the brand makes

1. Simple workflows will be won by self-serve products; complicated operational workflows need humans to discover, set up, deploy, and monitor.
2. Autonomy must be earned per job — approvals and audit trails are product, not friction — because rushed agents fail.
3. Therefore: consultants who own outcomes, on a platform built for supervision. Both arms, one company.

## Personality & voice

- **Persona:** the proven young operator — confidence from reps — who communicates like a lawyer.
- **Aaker mix:** competence first, excitement second (the open ground: premium peers run competence + sophistication; ServiceTitan runs neither), sophistication as finish only.
- **Anti-model:** ServiceTitan — "old style," "made their product dumb for their audience," "for the masses." Rule: digestible, never dumbed down.
- **Emotional engine:** "AI is the new internet, I can't get left behind" — era urgency delivered calmly, through evidence.
- **Voice bans (absolute):** hype vocabulary; bot/chatbot/copilot; exclamation marks; unexplained tech terms.
- **Primary objection to answer everywhere:** "this is too advanced for us." Every mechanism sentence pairs with a "we do this part" sentence. No new software, no migrations.

## Proof register (publishable today)

- Solar: permit cost $125 → $25 including labor (engagement closing).
- Property management: 150 → 250 doors per property manager via work-order triage + maintenance dispatch (engagement closing).
- Department-level case studies, outside the verticals but same job types: CodeGPT (upsell agents), Question Base (revenue increase), Keylead (content production up, costs down), NEOM.
- HVAC: no proof, no pipeline named — featured on direction. Page leads with department analogies until a deployment lands.
- Excluded: all legacy talent-program statistics.

## Site decisions made in the interview

1. Primary conversion: book an AI opportunity audit (consulting-first for 12 months).
2. Full Hive stack diagram on the site — corrects the documented misperception that Nairon is service-only.
3. Founder-led: Luka is the face; vision section makes the era argument, signed.
4. Hive presented industry-agnostic everywhere; vertical expression lives only in consultancy pages, imagery, case studies — because the win state is the platform company, with other consultancies performing service on Hive. Nairon's consultancy is the reference implementation.
